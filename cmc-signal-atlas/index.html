Scoring methodology

Advancing breadth · 35%Share of non-stablecoin top-100 assets reporting a positive 24h price change.
CMC Fear & Greed · 25%CMC's 0–100 market sentiment indicator; omitted and weight renormalized when unavailable.
Median momentum · 25%Rescales the sample median 24h return to 0–100 using 50 + 7 × median return, clamped.
Breadth alignment · 15%100 − 10 × absolute gap between equal-weighted and cap-weighted 24h returns, clamped.

Model choices are interpretive, not empirically validated predictors. Snapshot samples can differ from full-market statistics. Thresholds can be refined by users and must not be marketed as financial risk ratings.
