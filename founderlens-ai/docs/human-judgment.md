# Human judgment by design

**Agent's role:** Challenge assumptions and suggest customer-discovery experiments using user-provided excerpts. It does not verify URLs, contact prospects, or decide whether a startup should launch.

**Human's responsibility:** Evaluate each source, consider counter-evidence, correct the generated critique, choose **approve**, **approve with reservations**, or **reject**, and explain the next validation step.

**Enforcement:** Report export is disabled until a real nonempty model output is present, an outcome is chosen, at least 12 characters of rationale are entered, and limitations are explicitly acknowledged. Rejected drafts can be exported for accountability.

**No side effects:** All exports are local Markdown downloads. There is no automatic external communication, publishing, or lead outreach.
