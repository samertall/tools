# Surplus Signal — 2:25 demo storyboard

**Format:** 1920x1080 MP4 / 16:9 / 2 to 3 minutes. Use the actual app to record the steps below and upload publicly to YouTube or Vimeo before submission. The supplied walkthrough graphics, if present, are illustrative; a true screen capture of the running deployed app is preferred.

**00:00–00:15 — Problem.** "Good food is wasted when a donor cannot find a suitable recipient before it expires. The challenge is operational: not just who needs it, but who can safely and feasibly accept it in time."

**00:15–00:33 — Solution.** "Introducing Surplus Signal, a local-first food-rescue matching dashboard. These are fictional demonstration organizations and offers. The app does not arrange real collections."

**00:33–00:55 — Overview.** Show live inventory, eligible opportunities, schematically mapped donor/recipient nodes, and the sample-data disclaimer.

**00:55–01:22 — Matching engine.** Open Match engine, adjust the search radius, show the score, urgency, distance, priority and capacity. Mention that category and allergen restrictions are hard blocks rather than just score penalties.

**01:22–01:44 — Simulated allocation.** Open an eligible match and allocate 3 kg. Show the food weight and recipient capacity change and the recalculated matches.

**01:44–02:03 — Capture new supply.** Open Surplus inventory, add a new fictional food offer with category, kilograms, location, expiry, and allergens. Explain how it becomes eligible immediately.

**02:03–02:20 — Impact and measurement.** Open Impact studio and view the simulated allocation record and CSV export. Explain that environmental estimates are illustrative, not measured, and that actual pilot performance would be measured by verified kilograms safely delivered.

**02:20–02:25 — Close.** "Surplus Signal. Find the next home for usable food — before time runs out."
