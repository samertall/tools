06 / OPEN METHODS

No black box. No secret sauce.

01

Open, auditable data

English Premier League fixtures and final scores sourced from the OpenFootball public-domain dataset ↗. The browser retrieves the latest repository snapshot; the supplied offline subset is clearly labelled.

02

Explainable probabilities

Team home/away scoring and conceding rates are shrunk towards fixed league priors (six-match equivalent), then combined in an independent-Poisson model. We report the full home/draw/away distribution.

03

Transparent evaluation

Chronological rolling-origin backtesting compares multiclass Brier scores and log losses against a simple historical-league-frequency baseline, with same-day matches evaluated before updates.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONS

Not calibrated for betting, medical or financial decisions. Ignores team injuries, lineups, player-level quality, red cards, league point deductions and tactical systems. Historical results are not proof of future performance. Changing the home-attacking slider illustrates sensitivity, not causality.
